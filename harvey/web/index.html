Using FindClients

One product, researched prospects and manual conversations.

  1. Describe the product and target customer on the Leads page.
  2. Add a company, or find companies with Discover. Confirm the signals you want in Signals.
  3. Open a company card and run research. Review the source links, identity and exact excerpts.
  4. Add specific reviews, business owner replies or professional posts manually where the source cannot be read automatically.
  5. Check one relevant piece of evidence and draft an opening message from it. Edit and copy it for your own manual contact.
  6. Record actual contact and replies, or export checked prospect research for a client.

No email, SMS or social messages are sent. Research and drafted messages are not proof of buyer interest, qualified leads, meetings or revenue. An asking price is a note, not a payment.

Public sources can be wrong, incomplete or outdated. Avoid private life details and unsupported claims. A human check is your judgement, not independent verification. Review drafts before using them.

Keep costs small: company website research uses up to six pages and one AI selection call; each requested draft adds one AI call. Optional review/profile searches use two Serper queries. Search, discovery and provider limits still apply.
